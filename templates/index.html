The Science Behind AgriGuard

Spatio-Temporal Graph Neural Networks (ST-GNN) represent a paradigm shift in agricultural AI

Graph Neural Networks

Unlike traditional CNNs that treat images independently, GNNs model the relationships between different plants, creating a connected graph of your entire farm.

  • Node features: Plant health, soil conditions
  • Edge features: Proximity, irrigation patterns
  • Message passing between neighboring plants

Temporal Dynamics

Our models capture how diseases and pests evolve over time, learning patterns of spread that are invisible to single-time analysis.

  • LSTM and GRU layers for temporal memory
  • Attention mechanisms over time steps
  • Seasonal pattern recognition

Spatial Reasoning

Understanding how diseases spread across your field based on wind patterns, irrigation, and plant proximity.

  • Graph convolutional networks for spatial features
  • Distance-weighted adjacency matrices
  • Wind direction and terrain modeling

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about ST-GNN technology

What makes Graph Neural Networks better for agriculture?
Traditional CNNs analyze each image independently, missing the crucial relationships between plants. GNNs model your entire field as a connected graph, capturing how diseases spread from plant to plant through proximity, wind patterns, and irrigation systems. This graph-based approach achieves 2.3x better prediction accuracy for disease spread.
How does temporal modeling improve predictions?
By analyzing sequences of images over time, our models learn the progression patterns of diseases. For example, early blight follows characteristic temporal signatures that our LSTM layers can recognize before visual symptoms appear, giving you up to 48 hours of early warning.
What crops does AgriGuard support?
Currently, we support 20+ major crops including rice, wheat, corn, tomatoes, potatoes, cotton, sugarcane, and various fruits. Our graph-based approach generalizes well across different crop types, and we're continuously expanding our training data.
How many images do I need to upload?
For optimal graph construction, we recommend uploading 5-10 images from different sections of your field. This allows us to build a representative graph of your farm. You can upload up to 10 leaf images and 10 pest images per analysis.
Can I use AgriGuard offline in the field?
Yes! Our mobile-friendly interface works on any smartphone. Images are uploaded when you have connectivity, and predictions are processed on our servers. You'll receive SMS notifications with results even without data access.
